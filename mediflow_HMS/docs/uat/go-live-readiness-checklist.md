# Go-Live Readiness Checklist

## Application
- UAT sign-off complete for all critical workflows
- No open Sev 1/Sev 2 defects
- AI draft approval process validated

## Data and Infra
- Production database backup and restore drill completed
- Monitoring/alert channels verified
- Secrets rotated and injected via secure store

## Security
- RBAC spot checks completed by role
- Audit log traceability validated for sensitive operations
- TLS, ingress, and reverse-proxy rules verified

## Operations
- Runbook and rollback plan approved
- On-call roster confirmed for launch window
- Deployment pipeline dry-run successful

## Business Readiness
- Training sessions completed for each staff role
- Helpdesk escalation matrix distributed
- Hypercare window and support SLA communicated
