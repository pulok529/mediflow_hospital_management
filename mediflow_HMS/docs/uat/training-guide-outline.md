# Hospital Staff Training Guide Outline

## Session 1: Reception and Registration
- Patient registration
- Appointment booking and queue updates
- Handling common validation errors

## Session 2: Doctor and Nursing
- Consultation save workflow
- Nursing notes, vitals, and medication due list
- Shift handover and transfer/discharge prep

## Session 3: Diagnostics and Pharmacy
- Lab/radiology order lifecycle
- Inventory, dispensing, low-stock handling
- Safety checks for results and medicine handoff

## Session 4: Billing and Admin
- Billing lifecycle, discount queue, refunds
- Role and permission hygiene
- Audit log usage

## Session 5: AI Draft Workflow
- Draft generation by feature
- Human review/approval rules
- Safety labels and non-autonomous usage policy

## Assessment
- Role-based practical checklist completion
- Super-user sign-off per department
